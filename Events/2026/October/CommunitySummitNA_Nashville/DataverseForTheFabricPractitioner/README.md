## The talk

Dataverse isn't just another source. It's an application platform with its own rules, names, and relationships, and those rules travel with the data. This session walks through what Fabric practitioners need to understand before they build: understand the site, inspect the blueprint, connect the utilities, build for analytics, and pass inspection.
